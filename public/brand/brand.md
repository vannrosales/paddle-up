# PaddleUp Brand Guide

**Official Name:** Pickleball Players Profiling, Achievements, and Role  
**Display Name:** PaddleUp  
**Tagline:** Your pickleball profile, achievements, and role in one place.

## Color Tokens ("Court Glass")
- **Accent (Optic Ball Yellow-Green):** `#C8F31D` (Light) / `#D4FF3A` (Dark)
- **Accent Ink:** `#1C2A00` (Always dark contrast on accent)
- **Court Blue:** `#0A84FF`
- **Court Green:** `#34C759` (Light) / `#30D158` (Dark)
- **Kitchen Red:** `#FF453A`
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Light) / `#1C1C1E` (Dark)
- **Background:** `#F2F2F7` (Light) / `#000000` (Dark)

## Assets
- `mark.svg` — Apple squircle app tile with paddle silhouette and perforated optic ball.
- `logo.svg` — Primary horizontal lockup for light mode.
- `logo-dark.svg` — Horizontal lockup for dark mode.
- `icon.svg` — Modern responsive browser favicon.

## Rules
- Keep high contrast: always use dark text `#1C2A00` over the optic yellow accent.
- Do not distort or outline the ball perforations.
- Minimum clear space around mark: 16px equivalent.
